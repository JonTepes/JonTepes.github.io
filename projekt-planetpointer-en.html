Planet Pointer

  • Back to projects

A two-axis pointer that works out where in the sky a chosen planet is from its GPS location and the exact time, then points at it.

Video

How it works

  • Mechanics: a 3D-printed body and pointer driven by two motors: one for direction (azimuth), one for tilt.
  • Sensors: a GPS module provides location and time; a compass and an IMU on top of the pointer measure where it actually points.
  • Control: an ESP32 compares the computed and measured direction and corrects the pointer's position in closed loop.

Upgrade in progress: OmniPointer

The same pointer as a navigation device: pick a destination on a map in your phone's browser and send it over Bluetooth; the pointer shows the direction along the road or in a straight line. The firmware and web interface are written but not yet tested on the device.
