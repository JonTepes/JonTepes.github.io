RGB shadow lamp and working with materials

  • Back to projects

This project combines colour theory, 3D modelling and handcraft. The lamp has separate red, green and blue light sources that cast multi-coloured shadows (cyan, magenta and yellow) on objects.

Design and fabrication

  • 3D design: I designed the housing, arms and base in Fusion 360 and 3D printed them. The red, green and blue LEDs sit in a triangle, so each casts its shadow from a slightly different spot and the shadows take on colours.
  • Concrete-filled base: the 3D-printed base is filled with concrete to give the lamp weight and stability.
  • Mixed materials: in my projects I often combine 3D printing with composites (concrete, epoxy resin), acrylic and wood.
