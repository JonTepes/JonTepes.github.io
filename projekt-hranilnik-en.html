Smart pet feeder

  • Back to projects

This project makes automatic pet feeding reliable. Commercial feeders are often expensive or stop working when the internet goes down, so I designed and built my own from scratch.

Demo

How it works

  • Mechanism (3D printing): the enclosure and internals are fully designed in Fusion 360. A parametric auger keeps dry food (kibble) from jamming.
  • Electronics and reliability: an ESP32 with a real-time clock (RTC) module. The feeder needs no cloud connection and feeds on time even without internet.
  • Software: C++ firmware, written with help from AI tools. I spent most of the time on mechanics, fabrication and hardware integration.
