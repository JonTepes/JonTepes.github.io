I am a Master's student in Organization and Management of Information Systems. My passions are Industry 4.0, Internet of Things (IoT), and rapid prototyping. I specialize in designing mechanical parts in Fusion 360, integrating them with electronics (ESP32, Raspberry Pi), and utilizing AI-assisted development to rapidly build functional, real-world systems.

Featured Projects

IoT Digital Twin (Factory Model)

My degree project: A miniature Industry 4.0 factory with a 3D printed crane, conveyor belts, and color sorting sensors. Features real-time control via MQTT, Raspberry Pi, and a custom Node.js dashboard.

Smart IoT Pet Feeder

An automated feeder built from scratch. Features a jam-proof parametric mechanism designed in Fusion 360. Powered by an ESP32 and an RTC module for high reliability and deep-sleep energy efficiency.

ESPHome Smart Planter

An automated self-watering system built with ESP32 and ESPHome. It monitors soil moisture, triggers water pumps only when necessary, and utilizes deep sleep mode for maximum power efficiency.

RGB Shadow Lamp

A custom-built lamp that utilizes color theory to cast multi-colored shadows. This project showcases my hands-on experience with composite materials, including epoxy resin, acrylics, concrete, and woodworking.

Minor Projects & Experiments

Hardware Tinkering & Electronics

A showcase of my smaller builds, including custom RP2040 macro keypads, a low-latency Hitpad game controller, an API-integrated LED Matrix Clock, and a digital POV Zoetrope.
