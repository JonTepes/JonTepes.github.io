Macro keyboard with a custom PCB

  • Back to projects

A 20-key keyboard for shortcuts and macros, from PCB to case. It is the latest of the keyboards I have been designing since 2022.

Design

  • PCB: custom board with an ATmega32u4 microcontroller, a USB-C connector and a 5 × 4 key matrix with diodes. JLCPCB assembled the SMD parts; I added the switches and case.
  • Firmware: QMK with my own keyboard definition (matrix, pins, layout).
  • Case: custom 3D-printed case.
