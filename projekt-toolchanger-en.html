Tool changer for a 3D printer

  • Back to projects

Multi-colour printing on a regular printer means swapping filament by hand or wasting a lot of material. So I converted an Ender 3 Max to swap whole print heads automatically.

Video

Design

  • Mechanics: print-head carriers and docking (Anycubic Kobra 2 heads), designed in Fusion 360 and 3D printed.
  • Electronics and firmware: I moved the printer to Klipper and configured it for multiple print heads and automatic changes during a print.
  • Result: on calibration prints the prototype completed about 9 out of 10 tool changes.

Status

The project stayed a prototype: making it reliable enough for long prints would need a lot more development. The printer has since been taken apart, but the experience stayed: repeatable positioning, tolerances of 3D-printed parts and systematic testing.
