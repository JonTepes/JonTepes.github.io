Smart plant pot (ESPHome)

  • Back to projects

An automatic watering system for house plants that prevents both drying out and overwatering. A complete solution: from the enclosure and electronics to smart-home integration.

Key features

  • 3D design and printing: I designed the enclosure in Fusion 360 with room for the plant and a hidden tank for water and electronics.
  • Sensors and pump: an ESP32 measures soil moisture and the tank water level (ultrasonic sensor) and runs the pump only when the soil is too dry.
  • Low power: the device sleeps most of the time (deep sleep) and wakes only to measure or water.
  • Home Assistant: the firmware is built on ESPHome. Target moisture, sensor calibration, pump run time and sleep interval can be set remotely.

Custom PCB (new revision)

  • Design: a KiCad board with an ESP32-C3 SuperMini, powered by an 18650 Li-ion cell with USB-C charging (TP4056), cell protection (DW01A + FS8205A), a boost converter (MT3608) and a 3.3 V regulator. Connectors for the pump, moisture sensor and ultrasonic sensor.
  • Status: designed and ready for manufacturing; not built yet. Schematic (PDF)
